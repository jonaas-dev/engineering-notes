# Career

Notes on getting better at the job and on the parts of a career that are not technical:
what separates a senior from a junior, how promotion actually works, and how to keep doing
this for twenty years without burning out.

Notes whose body is not in English are marked `[ES]` / `[CA]`.

## Growing as an engineer

- [Advice for Junior Developers](advice-for-junior-developers.md) — code is not the point,
  maintenance is where the money goes, and the best code is the code you did not write.
- [Junior to senior: an action plan](junior-to-senior-an-action-plan.md) — connect engineering
  to revenue, and keep a brag document before you need one.
- [20 Things I've Learned in my 20 Years as a Software Engineer](20-things-ive-learned-in-my-20-years-as-a-software-engineer.md) —
  including the one worth repeating: avoid 0.1x programmers rather than hunt for 10x ones.
- [Mike Acton's Expectations of Professional Software Engineers](expectations-of-professional-software-engineers.md) —
  a long checklist of professional behaviours, annotated with what I already do and what I don't.
- [The 10x Programmer Myth](the-10x-programmer-myth.md) — the four archetypes that get mistaken
  for a 10x engineer: the code fountain, the point hog, the overcomplicator, the 80-percenter.
- [Why measuring experience in years is not good enough](why-measuring-experience-in-years-is-not-good-enough.md) —
  time served grows on its own; the experience that counts does not.
- [Breadth vs. depth, in which Dave supports job hopping](breadth-vs-depth-supporting-job-hopping.md) —
  why widening the toolbox beats deepening one tool, past a point.
- [How trying new programming languages helped me grow](how-trying-new-programming-languages-helped-me-grow.md) —
  on leaving the bubble of your daily language and being junior again on purpose.
- [3 Things You Need to Succeed as a Software Developer](3-things-you-need-to-succeed-as-a-software-developer.md) —
  technical skills, soft skills, mindset, and why the third is the one nobody audits.
- [¿Quieres ser desarrollador senior? Haz esto](quieres-ser-desarrollador-senior-haz-esto.md) `[ES]` —
  following the tutorial is not enough; go to the official documentation.
- [4 habilidades que impulsarán tu carrera](4-habilidades-que-impulsaran-tu-carrera.md) `[CA]` —
  very readable code, a systematic post-mortem habit, and being able to justify a decision.

## Working on real systems

- [Alien artefacts](alien-artefacts.md) — the subcategory of legacy code written by very smart
  people who have left: it works, and it resists change. Includes how not to create one.
- [How to survive your project's first 100,000 lines](how-to-survive-your-projects-first-100000-lines.md) —
  drench the code in assertions, lean on the type system, leave clues rather than comments.
- [How to Build Software like an SRE](how-to-build-software-like-an-sre.md) — no in-code config
  fallbacks, strict RPC settings, never give up on local testing, avoid state.
- [Top 10 Things You Should Avoid While Coding](10-things-you-should-avoid-while-coding.md) —
  a short checklist, useful mostly as a reminder of the boring failure modes.
- [Non-technical tips to ease the stress of debugging](tips-to-ease-the-stress-of-debugging.md) —
  reproduce first, and treat debugging as a process rather than an emergency.
